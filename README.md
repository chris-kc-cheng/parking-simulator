# Parking Simulator

Learn the feel of parking from the driver's seat with a multi-view driving
simulator built for practice.

## [Play Parking Simulator](https://chris-kc-cheng.github.io/parking-simulator/)

## Choose a lesson

- **Reverse park** — back into a highlighted parking bay.
- **Front-in** — approach and enter the bay nose-first.
- **Parallel** — practice positioning the car alongside the curb.

Choose a lesson from the top of the windshield. Your goal is to finish with the
vehicle completely inside the yellow parking lines.

## Drive

| Action | Keyboard | On-screen control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steer left or right | <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> | Hold the left or right arrow |
| Drive forward | <kbd>↑</kbd> | Hold **Drive** |
| Reverse | <kbd>↓</kbd> | Hold **Reverse** |
| Select a gear | — | Choose **P**, **R**, **N**, or **D** on the dashboard |

The speed display and steering wheel move with the car. Select **Reset** at any
time to return to the lesson's starting position.

## Use the driving aids

Watch the side mirrors and rear-view mirror as you maneuver. The rear camera
includes parking guides, while the bird's-eye camera helps you judge the car's
position between the lines. Either camera panel can be hidden and restored.

Open the settings button to adjust the mirror fisheye and camera field of view.
Touch controls are available automatically on phones and tablets.
